# Finix Academy — Course Delivery Plan, Time Model & Pricing

Generated from live database measurements (not estimates): 3 tracks, 22 modules,
83 lessons, 44,737 English words + full Arabic (219k chars, Egyptian register),
330 quiz questions across 66 quizzes (Bronze/Silver/Gold per module),
35 original SVG diagrams, 27 verified YouTube resources.

Time model per module: reading at 130 wpm (technical content, bilingual
audience) + attached videos at ~9 min each + 3 quiz tiers (~37 min) + 20%
practice/reflection overhead.

## Recommended session plan (blended: instructor-led + platform)

Session = 2.5 h classroom/online, trainees do platform reading + Bronze quiz
before each session; Silver/Gold quizzes after.

the 24.5 h trainee total; sessions revisit the hard 40%).

### Calendar options
- **Intensive:** 3 sessions/week → full programme in 4 weeks.
- **Standard (recommended):** 2 sessions/week → 6 weeks.
- **Evening/working technicians:** 1 session/week → 12 weeks.
- **Self-paced (no instructor):** 24.5 h content ≈ 4–6 weeks at 4–6 h/week.

### Strongly recommended addition: practical lab days (not yet in the course)
- Lab A (after Track 1): multimeter work, breaker sizing, cable checks — 4 h
- Lab B (after Track 2): live SONOFF install, pairing, mesh diagnosis — 4 h
- Lab C (after Track 3): DOL starter wiring → star-delta conversion → fault
  injection on a real panel — 6 h

## Pricing recommendation (Egypt market, 2026)

Anchors: professional PLC/automation courses at Egyptian training centres run
3,500–9,000 EGP; vendor-certification courses 4,000–12,000 EGP. This course is
bilingual, vendor-backed (authorized SONOFF distributor), with tiered
certification — price at the upper-middle of that band.

| Offer | Contents | Recommended price |
|---|---|---|
| Single track, self-paced | platform + quizzes + certificate | **1,900 EGP** |
| Single track, instructor-led | + 4 sessions + exam proctoring | **3,500 EGP** |
| Full programme, self-paced | all 3 tracks | **4,500 EGP** (vs 5,700 separate) |
| Full programme, instructor-led | 12 sessions + 3 certs | **8,500 EGP** |
| Full programme + lab days | + Labs A/B/C, materials incl. | **11,900 EGP** |
| Corporate (per seat, ≥5) | instructor-led programme | **6,800 EGP/seat** |

Rationale:
- Self-paced at ~45% of instructor-led keeps the platform accessible while
  protecting classroom revenue.
- The 8,500 EGP flagship ≈ price of one mid-range SONOFF project's hardware —
  positions training as a business investment for installers.
- Labs priced +3,400 covers components, consumables and bench time.
- Early-bird (−15%) for the first cohort; SONOFF-hardware buyers get a
  1,000 EGP voucher — training drives distribution and vice versa.

## Certification structure (already implemented in-platform)
- Bronze (pass 80%): knowledge check per module — free retakes.
- Silver: applied scenarios.
- Gold: full-module mastery; required for track certificate.
- Track certificate on all module Golds + track exam in final session.
